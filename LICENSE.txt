Local operation and online functions
-----------------------------------

GPU control, monitoring and profile handling run locally. Online functions
include application and game-library updates, community profile services,
sensor-support downloads, game requests and diagnostic/report uploads.
These functions communicate with the respective online services; the
services receive the connection information and request data needed to
provide the requested function.

Diagnostic exports are saved locally. Sharing a diagnostic package or
submitting a report is a separate user action. Review the included data
before uploading it or sharing it with another person.

Experimental settings — use at your own risk
-------------------------------------------

Experimental controls have not been validated on every GPU or driver.
In particular, "Expert voltage settings" can change XBAR/SYS clocks and
voltage-demand offsets, video clock, Core/Fabric voltage limits and Voltage
Boost where supported. These settings apply globally to the selected GPU,
including outside games. Voltage demand is not a guarantee of a fixed
physical voltage. Enabling access alone applies no tuning; applying values
requires the separate "Apply globally" action.

DCC's "NVIDIA Experimental" mode (Power Efficiency Mode) is a separate
experimental feature from "Expert voltage settings". Both are covered by
these risk and liability provisions.
